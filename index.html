ABOUT US & OUR CRAFT

Dedication of Cirebon Artisans Reaching World Markets

Cirebon is recognized as a global center of the rattan industry, processing more than 80% of raw rattan in Indonesia. Pak Ridwan has dedicated over 12 years to combining the hand-weaving expertise of senior Tegalwangi artisans with contemporary designs of high aesthetic value.

All of our products go through controlled kiln-drying processes (moisture below 12% to prevent mold), eco-friendly anti-borer treatment, and water-based clear coat protection that meets international furniture safety regulations.
